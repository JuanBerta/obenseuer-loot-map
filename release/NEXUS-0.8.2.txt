=== FILE DESCRIPTION ===
Obenseuer-LootMap-BepInEx6-v0.8.2.zip : Loot Map 0.8.2 (in-game mod, BepInEx 6). Extract into the Obenseuer folder.

=== NEXUS CHANGELOG: Loot Map 0.8.2 ===
- New: a What to craft tab in the big window (Home key) recommends what to craft for money with the skills you have: the best recipes by profit per hour and by profit per craft at real shop prices, using your live skill levels, and a Worth levelling for list of recipes up to three levels above yours that would pay more per hour, priced at the level that unlocks them. It can be limited to one skill, hides recipes whose ingredients no shop sells unless you include them, and leaves recipes under a minute out of the per-hour list. Each entry has Craft and Find buttons.
- Website: a new What to craft page with the same three lists, based on the levels you set on the Your skills page.
- Fix: the items fixed in place on shop shelves and market shelves were read as item 0 (the extractor took an empty id field instead of the real one), so many shelves showed nothing. They are now read correctly in 75 scenes. For example the O-Market on Trade Road now lists 176 items instead of 91 (Osmo Olut, cheese, packaged meat, baking yeast, cigarettes, cleaning products, frozen food and more), so the profit finder and the Craft plan can buy them there.

- New: every recipe now lists only the workstations that can really make it. A recipe needs a station of its type with the modifiers it requires and none it excludes (for example Counterfeit Money only works at a Printing Press, Special). The Craft plan shows each usable station with its quality, modifiers, real chance of success, profit and profit per hour, where it is sold and its price, and a Use button to pick the station for that recipe (the choice is saved, setting StationPicks). The profit finder, What to craft and the success chance use the station you picked, or the best quality one until you pick. Before, the best station of the type was assumed even when its modifiers did not match.
- Website: recipe pages and the shopping list got the same station picker and per-station success, profit and profit per hour.
- Limit: modifiers added by station upgrades are not in the data; two recipes have no station with the right modifiers, so all stations of their type are listed.

- New: a Copy inventory for the website button (in the shopping list panel, under Paste list from the website) copies what you carry. Paste it on the website's new What can I craft now page to see which recipes you can make right now and which are one ingredient short.
- Website: new pages for What can I craft now, a Skill levelling guide (fastest and cheapest recipes to the next level, using the game's own skill-gain formula), a Tenement cost calculator, a Loot ranking, a Power planner and a Patch changes page.
